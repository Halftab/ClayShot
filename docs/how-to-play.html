GETTING STARTED

How to play

ClayShot is about tracking a moving clay target, lining up your aim and choosing the right moment to shoot.

A simple routine

  1. Watch the target as it launches and follow its movement.
  2. Move your aim to lead the target rather than chasing its last position.
  3. Fire when your aim and timing come together.

Controls

ClayShot supports desktop and mobile input. The exact control prompts are shown in the game build. On compatible mobile devices, motion-based aiming can be used when motion access is available and enabled.

Tip: If motion aiming is available on your device, hold the phone comfortably and make small, deliberate movements.
